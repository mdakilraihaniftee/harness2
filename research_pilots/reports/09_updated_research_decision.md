# Updated research decision after deterministic phase 2

## Executive summary

The original composer bottleneck is now diagnosed and the compact interface is deterministically ready, but no additional paid model call was authorized in this phase. Therefore the compact interface has not yet demonstrated that it resolves the live bottleneck, and neither behavioral hypothesis can be reclassified from blocked.

The original failures were caused by pre-inference TLS setup, a secondary metadata-auth path, and—after those were fixed—filesystem navigation until OpenCode's step limit. None of the saved original responses made the required explicit composition decision. The new interface preserves exact evidence and patches in 668–1,518 estimated tokens, and future instrumentation will retain complete tool events.

## Required research classifications

| Idea | Classification | Recommendation | Actual evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAVE-R | **BLOCKED** | **Refine toward diagnostic evidence availability** | Injection and positive control are valid; systematic dependence is non-identifiable without provenance; no completed harmful LLM adoption exists. |
| CITE-R | **BLOCKED** | **Focus the next test on synergy plus interference** | Deterministic A+B synergy/C interference exists; no completed LLM composition judgment or real-executor interaction exists. |

These are recommendations for the next experiment, not authorization to implement either method.

## What would change the conclusions

For PAVE-R, completed clean/isolated/systematic decisions must show that systematic repeated errors increase unnecessary or harmful edits, ideally with an explicit repeated-evidence justification. A truthful provenance-only intervention should then change the decision without revealing fault. If completed systematic runs remain no worse than controls, the failure is unsupported in the tested setting and PAVE-R should be deprioritized or redirected to renderer reliability.

For CITE-R, E1 must make an explicit valid decision with A and B visibly available. If it constructs A+B, the simple composition-failure hypothesis is unsupported for the compact setting. If it rejects a useful edit because the individual execution failed, E2 must still prove H0/HA/HB fail and HAB succeeds under the real LLM executor. Only a completed E1/E2 pattern justifies testing C and selective bundle search.

## Smallest justified next step

Run exactly one controlled CITE E1 compact composer stage, capped at $1 and two actual calls. Inspect its raw tool trace, explicit notes, and tree before authorizing anything else. This is the cheapest test of whether the interface—not a new adaptation algorithm—removes the bottleneck.
